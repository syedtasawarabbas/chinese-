# HUM 434 Pinyin Studio
 
A free study tool for **Chinese Language (HUM 434)** at COMSATS University Islamabad, Lahore Campus, covering **Lecture 4 through Lecture 10**. It was built entirely from the course's own lecture slides and in-class note photos — nothing is pulled from outside sources.
 
No installation, login or setup is needed. Open the link on a phone or laptop and start studying.
 
## What it's for
 
The exam for this course follows a fixed paper pattern:
1. Pinyin → English translation
2. English → Pinyin translation
3. Vocabulary: Chinese to English
4. Missing tones (pinyin words with the tone marks removed)
The app gives you one dedicated practice mode for each of these, plus a lecture-by-lecture walkthrough to learn the material in the first place.
 
## Features
- **Lectures** — Work through Lecture 4 to Lecture 10 in order, exactly as they were taught. Lecture 4 includes the pronunciation rules (initials, finals, tones) and the counting slide, each illustrated with a real example word drawn from the actual course material. Every lecture's vocabulary list is paired, wherever possible, with a genuine example sentence from that lecture's slide or dialogue photos.
- **Quick Reference** — A single searchable table of every word across all seven lectures, by pinyin or English.
- **Q1 / Q2 — Translation drills** — Flip through real course sentences in both directions (pinyin → English and English → pinyin), with a "Knew it / Missed it" tracker that resurfaces anything you missed. Each answer also breaks the sentence down word by word with its own gloss.
- **Q3 — Vocabulary flashcards** — Drill every vocabulary word from Lectures 4–10, reversible (Chinese→English or English→Chinese).
- **Q4 — Missing tones** — Pinyin is shown without its tone marks; click each syllable to cycle through tones 1–4, neutral, and blank, then check your answer against the correct form. Works on both single words and full sentences.
## A note on sources
 
Everything in this tool is labeled by where it came from, so you always know what you're studying:
 
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **From your slide** | Transcribed directly from the lecture PDF |
| **From your note photos** | Transcribed from the in-class dialogue photos |
| **Reference** | Not legible in the source material — standard/general content included for completeness, flagged so you can verify it against your own notes |
| **Practice line** | Written to give extra drill practice; not from the original lecture |
 
A few known gaps are called out directly in the app (for example, where a slide photo was too blurry to read with full confidence, or where two photos seemed to disagree on a date). If you spot something that doesn't match your own notes or your instructor's slides, trust your notes first.
 
## For students
 
- Start on the **Lectures** tab and go through them in order if you're learning the material for the first time.
- Use **Q4 (Missing Tones)** early and often — tone accuracy is usually where marks are lost.
- Use the **Quick Reference** search when you just need to look one word up fast.
- The app remembers nothing between visits — there's no login and no progress is saved, so it's safe to open anytime without setup.
